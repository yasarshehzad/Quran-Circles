<h1 align="center">Quran Circles</h1>

<p align="center">
A private small-group Quran reflection app designed to help families, friends, and communities stay consistent with the Quran together.
</p>

<p align="center">
Built for the Quran.Foundation Hackathon.
</p>

<hr />

<h2>About</h2>

<p>
Quran Circles is a private, async-first Quran habit app focused on small trusted groups rather than public social feeds.
</p>

<p>
Users can create private circles with family, friends, spouses, or halaqah groups, follow one shared ayah each day, reflect in their own time, share short takeaways, and build a shared streak together.
</p>

<p>
The app is designed to support long-term Quran consistency beyond periods of short-term motivation such as Ramadan.
</p>

<hr />

<h2>Core Features</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Private Quran reflection circles</li>
  <li>Shared daily ayah experience</li>
  <li>Async participation across different schedules and timezones</li>
  <li>Group accountability through shared streaks</li>
  <li>Daily reflections and takeaways</li>
  <li>Audio recitation playback</li>
  <li>Translation support</li>
  <li>Bookmarks and saved reflections</li>
  <li>Shared-device family mode</li>
  <li>Individual-device and hybrid participation modes</li>
</ul>

<hr />

<h2>Participation Modes</h2>

<h3>Shared-Device Mode</h3>
<p>
Designed for families using one phone or tablet together, especially younger children with parents.
</p>

<h3>Individual Mode</h3>
<p>
Each member participates independently from their own device and account.
</p>

<h3>Hybrid Mode</h3>
<p>
Supports groups that sometimes meet together in person and sometimes participate remotely throughout the week.
</p>

<hr />

<h2>Core Idea</h2>

<p>
The central mechanic of Quran Circles is shared accountability.
</p>

<p>
A circle’s daily streak only continues when all required members complete that day’s reflection.
</p>

<p>
The goal is to encourage gentle consistency through trusted community rather than pressure or public social engagement.
</p>

<hr />

<h2>Quran Foundation API Integration</h2>

<h3>Content APIs Used</h3>

<ul>
  <li>Verse API</li>
  <li>Translation API</li>
  <li>Audio API</li>
</ul>

<h3>User APIs Used</h3>

<ul>
  <li>OAuth2 / OpenID Connect (OIDC)</li>
  <li>Rooms-related APIs</li>
  <li>Posts-related APIs</li>
  <li>Bookmarks API</li>
  <li>Streak Tracking APIs</li>
</ul>

<p>
Quran Circles integrates Quran Foundation APIs to power daily ayah loading, translations, recitation playback, authenticated user identity, reflections, bookmarks, and group accountability features.
</p>

<h2>Project Goal</h2>

<p>
The goal of Quran Circles is to explore how small trusted groups can help people maintain a deeper and more consistent relationship with the Quran through reflection, shared habits, and community support.
